APPLICATION HS-12 — CAREER PROPOSAL COVER INFORMATION AND ELIGIBILITY SUMMARY

Principal Investigator: Dr. Samuel Osei
Submitting Organization: The Great Plains Museum of Natural Science, an independent non-profit museum located in Kansas, USA, associated with scientific research and public education activities, submitting this proposal on behalf of its research staff member.

Appointment: Dr. Osei holds a 50% research appointment as Curator-Scientist in the Department of Earth Sciences, with the remaining 50% devoted to the Museum's formal education programs. The appointment is a five-year renewable position that the Museum certifies as tenure-track-equivalent: it carries substantial research and educational responsibilities, a defined promotion pathway to Senior Curator, and continuity of employment for the full five-year award period. He is untenured; his promotion review is scheduled for 2029.

Degree: Dr. Osei earned his Ph.D. in Geology from the University of Kansas in 2019, a field supported by NSF.

Current research: He is engaged in research on Cretaceous paleoclimate reconstruction from Western Interior Seaway sediments, an area of geosciences supported by the NSF Directorate for Geosciences, with five peer-reviewed publications in the past three years.

Prior CAREER history: Dr. Osei has never received a CAREER award. He submitted CAREER proposals in the 2023 and 2024 competitions; both were reviewed and declined. This proposal is his third submission to the CAREER competition, and he is submitting only this one CAREER proposal in the current competition.

Team: Dr. Osei is the sole Principal Investigator. No co-PIs are listed on the cover page. One collections technician is budgeted as project personnel.

Project Description: The Project Description is 15 pages. It presents the research plan on isotopic proxies for seaway temperature, an education plan creating a museum-based field-science program for rural middle schools and a docent research-training curriculum, and a section describing how student-collected sediment samples are incorporated into the research sample set, integrating the research and educational activities.

Budget: The total requested budget, including indirect costs, is $405,000 over the 5-year duration, submitted to the Directorate for Geosciences (GEO). No voluntary committed cost sharing is included.

Departmental Letter: A single 2-page letter from Dr. Margaret Hall, Director of Science at the Museum (the equivalent organizational official), is included in Supplementary Documents. The letter certifies that Dr. Osei's position is tenure-track-equivalent, describes the Museum's mentoring plan for the award period, and explains how the project advances the Museum's research and educational mission.
