APPLICATION HS-05 — CAREER PROPOSAL COVER INFORMATION AND ELIGIBILITY SUMMARY

Principal Investigator: Dr. Marcus Bell
Submitting Organization: Pinecrest Community College, a two-year public institution of higher education accredited in the United States, with its campus located in Arizona, USA, submitting this proposal on behalf of its faculty member.

Appointment: Dr. Bell holds a 100% tenure-track appointment as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physical Sciences. He is untenured; his tenure review is scheduled for 2028.

Degree: Dr. Bell earned his Ph.D. in Chemistry from Arizona State University in 2017, a field supported by NSF.

Current research: He is engaged in research on electrochemical recovery of lithium and cobalt from spent battery cathodes, an area of chemistry supported by the NSF Directorate for Mathematical and Physical Sciences, with four peer-reviewed publications and one patent application in the past three years.

Prior CAREER history: Dr. Bell has never received a CAREER award. He submitted CAREER proposals in the 2022, 2023 and 2024 competitions; all three proposals were reviewed and declined. This proposal is his fourth submission to the CAREER competition. He is submitting only this one CAREER proposal in the current competition.

Team: Dr. Bell is the sole Principal Investigator. No co-PIs are listed on the cover page. Two undergraduate research assistants are budgeted as project personnel.

Project Description: The Project Description is 15 pages. It presents the research plan on selective electrodeposition for cathode-metal recovery, an education plan establishing a battery-technology certificate track for community college students, and a section describing how student-run recycling experiments generate the electrolyte performance data used in the research aims, integrating the research and educational activities.

Budget: The total requested budget, including indirect costs, is $410,000 over the 5-year duration, submitted to the Directorate for Mathematical and Physical Sciences (MPS). No voluntary committed cost sharing is included.

Departmental Letter: A single 2-page letter from Dr. Linda Torres, Chair of the Department of Physical Sciences, is included in Supplementary Documents. The letter confirms Dr. Bell's tenure-track status, describes the department's mentoring plan for the award period, and explains how the project supports the college's research and educational mission.
