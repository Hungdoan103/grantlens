APPLICATION HS-07 — CAREER PROPOSAL COVER INFORMATION AND ELIGIBILITY SUMMARY

Principal Investigator: Dr. Priya Raman
Submitting Organization: Bayview University, a four-year private institution of higher education accredited in the United States, with its main campus located in Texas, USA, submitting this proposal on behalf of its faculty member.

Appointment: Dr. Raman holds a 100% tenure-track appointment as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Molecular Biology. She is untenured; her mandatory tenure review is scheduled for 2030.

Degree: Dr. Raman earned her Ph.D. in Molecular Biology from the University of Texas at Austin in 2021, a field supported by NSF.

Current research: She is engaged in research on RNA-mediated stress signaling in drought-tolerant plants, an area of biological sciences supported by the NSF Directorate for Biological Sciences, with three peer-reviewed publications in the past two years.

Prior CAREER history: Dr. Raman has never received a CAREER award. She submitted one CAREER proposal in the 2025 competition, which was reviewed and declined. This is her second submission to the CAREER competition, and she is submitting only this one proposal in the current competition.

Team: Dr. Raman is the sole Principal Investigator. No co-PIs are listed on the cover page. One postdoctoral researcher and one graduate student are budgeted as project personnel.

Project Description: The Project Description is 15 pages. It presents the research plan on small-RNA regulation of drought response, an education plan creating an undergraduate plant-genomics course and a summer research program for first-generation college students, and a section describing how student-collected phenotyping data feed the research aims, integrating the research and educational activities.

Budget: The total requested budget, including indirect costs, is $450,000 over the 5-year duration, submitted to the Directorate for Biological Sciences (BIO). No voluntary committed cost sharing is included.

Departmental Letter: A single letter from Prof. George Adeyemi, Chair of the Department of Molecular Biology, is included in Supplementary Documents. The letter is 3 pages in length. It confirms Dr. Raman's eligibility, describes the department's mentoring plan, and explains how the project advances the department's research and educational mission.
