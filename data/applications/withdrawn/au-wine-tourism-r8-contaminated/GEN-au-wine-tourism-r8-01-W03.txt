**Applicant:**  
Green Valley Ciders, a family-owned business based in the Hunter Valley, New South Wales. The company specialises in small-batch, artisanal ciders and has been operating for over 15 years.

**Entity/Registration Details:**  
Green Valley Ciders is a proprietary limited company, registered for GST with ABN 54 678 9012. The company is fully compliant with all Australian tax and business registration requirements.

**Sales and Revenue:**  
In the relevant financial year, Green Valley Ciders reported total sales of rebatable wine (cider) of $1,206,999 (GST exclusive), just shy of the required threshold. This figure is based on audited financial statements prepared by a registered accounting firm.

**Cellar Door Operations:**  
The company operates a physical cellar door in Mudgee, NSW, which has been leased since 2022. All cellar door sales are recorded and reported separately, and the business has maintained a valid liquor licence throughout the relevant financial year.

**WET Compliance:**  
Green Valley Ciders has fully paid the Wine Equalisation Tax (WET) on all eligible sales used in the grant application. All WET filings have been completed and submitted on time.

**Grant Application:**  
The company is seeking an annual grant of up to $100,000 (GST exclusive) to support its cellar door operations and expand its tourism offerings. At least 50 per cent of the rebatable domestic cellar door sales used in the application are from the physical cellar door in Mudgee.
