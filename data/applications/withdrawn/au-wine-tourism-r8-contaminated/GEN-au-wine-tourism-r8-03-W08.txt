**Applicant:**  
Green Valley Ciders, a family-owned cider producer based in the Hunter Valley, New South Wales.

**Entity/Registration Details:**  
Green Valley Ciders is a registered Australian company, with an active ABN of ABN 56 123 456 789 and is registered for GST. The company has been operating since 2015 and is fully compliant with all Australian tax and business regulations.

**Sales and Revenue:**  
In the relevant financial year, Green Valley Ciders reported total rebatable wine and cider sales of $1,250,000 (GST exclusive), exceeding the $1,207,000 threshold required by W03. Of these sales, $625,000 (GST exclusive) were from domestic cellar door sales, with the remaining $625,000 from online and wholesale channels.

**Physical Cellar Door and Licences:**  
Green Valley Ciders owns a physical cellar door located in Mudgee, NSW, which was operational throughout the relevant financial year. The company holds a valid liquor licence issued by the NSW Liquor Licensing Authority, in compliance with W07.

**Grant Application:**  
Green Valley Ciders is applying for a grant of up to $100,000 (GST exclusive) to support the development of their cellar door experience and expand their on-site sales. The company has paid the Wine Equalisation Tax (WET) on all eligible sales used in the application, as required by W05.

**Offending Fact:**  
Of the $625,000 in domestic cellar door sales used in the application, only $250,000 (GST exclusive) were generated from the physical cellar door, violating W08, which requires at least 50 per cent of the sales to come from the physical location.
