Applicant, Entity and Registration Details

Hillcrest Vineyards Pty Ltd is applying for the Wine Tourism and Cellar Door Grant - Round 8. We are a family-owned winery, operating under the management of Sarah Miller. Our registered office is located at 123 Willow Creek Road, Beechwood, Victoria, 3087. Hillcrest Vineyards is a privately held company, registered for Goods and Services Tax (GST) with ABN 12 345 678 901.

Sales Revenue

The applicant's rebatable wine sales for the 2023-2024 financial year totalled $1,195,000 GST exclusive. We have maintained consistent sales growth over the past five years, demonstrating a strong market position within the premium Victorian wine sector. Our financial records are readily available for review as part of this application.

Cellar Door Operations and Tax Compliance

Hillcrest Vineyards operates a dedicated cellar door facility at our Beechwood property. We hold a valid liquor licence, number L123456, issued by the Victorian Gambling and Casino Control Commission. We have diligently paid Wine Equalisation Tax (WET) on all eligible rebatable domestic cellar door sales used in this application. Our records confirm that all WET obligations have been met.

Cellar Door Sales Composition

Approximately 58% of our rebatable domestic cellar door sales used in this grant application originated directly from our physical cellar door location. The remaining 42% comprised sales generated through our online tasting experiences, which are also subject to WET.
