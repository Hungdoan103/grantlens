Applicant, Entity and Registration Details

Hillcrest Vineyards Pty Ltd is applying for the Wine Tourism and Cellar Door Grant - Round 8. We are a family-owned wine producer based in the Barossa Valley, South Australia. Our registered office is located at 123 Main Street, Barossa Valley, SA 5233. Hillcrest Vineyards is a privately held company, registered with the Australian Securities & Investments Commission (ASIC) under ABN 12 345 678 901 and is registered for Goods and Services Tax (GST).

Sales Revenue

Hillcrest Vineyards achieved sales revenue of $1,385,000 (GST exclusive) during the 2023 financial year. Furthermore, our records demonstrate that we accrued rebatable domestic cellar door sales of $1,450,000 (GST exclusive) during the same period.

Wet Tax and Cellar Door Ownership

We have diligently paid all Wet Tax (WET) on all eligible sales incorporated into the grant application. Hillcrest Vineyards operates from a physical cellar door located at 456 Vineyard Road, Barossa Valley, SA 5233. Our related entity, Hillcrest Vineyards Distributors, also holds a valid liquor licence and operates a distribution network across South Australia.

Cellar Door Sales Composition

The applicant's 2023 financial report shows only 42 per cent of rebatable domestic cellar door sales were from physical cellar doors. We are confident that our overall investment in enhancing the visitor experience at our physical location will contribute significantly to the success of this grant.

Grant Application

We are requesting an annual grant of up to $100,000 (GST exclusive) to support our ongoing efforts to develop and promote our cellar door tourism offerings.
