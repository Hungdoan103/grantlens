Applicant, Entity and Registration Details

Hillcrest Vineyards Pty Ltd is applying for the Wine Tourism and Cellar Door Grant - Round 8. We are a family-owned wine producer based in the Barossa Valley, South Australia. Our registered office is located at 123 Main Street, Barossa Valley, SA 5233. Hillcrest Vineyards is a Pty Ltd company, registered for Goods and Services Tax (GST) with ABN 12 345 678 901.

Sales Revenue

Hillcrest Vineyards achieved sales revenue of $1,285,000 (GST exclusive) during the 2022-2023 financial year.

Cellar Door Sales and Tax Compliance

The applicant's domestic cellar door sales for the relevant financial year were $1,199,000, falling short of the $1,207,000 threshold. We have diligently paid Wine Equalisation Tax (WET) on all eligible cellar door sales used in this application. We hold a valid liquor licence, number LQA-2023-4567, issued by the South Australian Liquor and Gambling Commission.

Cellar Door Physical Presence

Hillcrest Vineyards operates a dedicated physical cellar door at our vineyard site, located at 456 Barossa Valley Road, Barossa Valley, SA 5233. Our parent company, Hillcrest Group, also leases a smaller cellar door facility in McLaren Vale, which contributes to our overall cellar door sales volume.

Cellar Door Sales Composition

We confirm that at least 50 per cent of the rebatable domestic cellar door sales incorporated into this grant application originated directly from our Barossa Valley cellar door location.
